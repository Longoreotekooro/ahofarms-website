EVERY BATCH TESTED

What goes into every Certificate of Analysis

Every batch of Aho Farms medicinal flower is independently tested by Hill Labs before supply to licensed prescribers and pharmacies.

Cannabinoid Profile

Quantification of key cannabinoids across the full batch spectrum for prescriber reference.

Terpene Analysis

Identification and quantification of primary and secondary terpenes unique to each batch and cultivar.

Heavy Metals

Screening for lead, mercury, arsenic, cadmium, and other regulated heavy metal contaminants.

Pesticide Residues

Comprehensive multi-residue panel testing against New Zealand and international MRL standards.

Microbial Testing

Testing for total aerobic count, E. coli, Salmonella, yeasts, moulds, and other microbial contaminants.

Moisture & Foreign Matter

Water activity and moisture content measurement, alongside physical inspection for foreign matter.

Testing partner: Hill Labs, Hamilton IANZ Accredited

FOR PATIENTS

Talk to your doctor about medicinal cannabis

If you are a patient interested in medicinal cannabis, the right first step is a conversation with your doctor or a specialist cannabis clinic. Aho Farms products are prescription medicines — they are not available for purchase without a valid prescription from a registered prescriber.

The following organisations can help you access information and connect with prescribers experienced in medicinal cannabis.
