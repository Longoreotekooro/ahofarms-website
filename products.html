    05 · QUALITY

    Quality doesn't begin at the laboratory.

    Our growers are the first line of quality control.

    Plant health and defects are identified during cultivation, not at final testing. Flower is harvested and cured with care, hand-trimmed by experienced cannabis trimmers, and passes through multiple quality-control touchpoints before batch testing.

    20+ Years of combined cultivation experience within our growing team.
    Our approach to quality
    • Growers firstIssues are seen and acted on in the crop, by the people who know it.
    • Harvest and cureSlow, deliberate post-harvest handling protects what the plant built.
    • Hand trimmedExperienced trimmers finish every flower by hand.
    • Batch testedIndependent testing on every batch, with the record to prove it.
