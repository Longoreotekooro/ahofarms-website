02 / THE WHENUA
TŌ MĀTOU WHENUA · THE WHENUA

Hawke's Bay

Our growing sites are in the Hawke's Bay region of Aotearoa New Zealand. The land is Māori freehold land, farmed in partnership with the Waaka whānau. Hawke's Bay is one of New Zealand's sunniest regions — averaging over 2,200 sunshine hours per year, with abundant natural sunlight and intense UV exposure. Outdoor and greenhouse growing harness that natural advantage for a richer terpene profile and a more complex cannabinoid expression; indoor growing adds the precision and environmental control that keeps every batch consistent. The soil here has been farmed for generations. We add to that legacy by treating it with the same respect.

    04 / HOW WE GROW

    Cultivation principles

    No synthetic inputs

    No synthetic pesticides, herbicides, or artificial growth enhancers. We manage pests through integrated pest management and natural inputs.

    Soil health first

    We monitor soil health through the season and make management decisions based on long-term land health, not short-term yield.

    Natural light cycles

    Outdoor and greenhouse cannabis follows Hawke's Bay's natural photoperiod. This produces a more complex and authentic terpene and cannabinoid profile.

    Full chain of custody

    From seed selection through harvest, drying, trimming, packaging, and laboratory testing — every step is documented.

    05 / QUALITY ASSURANCE
    TE WHAKAMĀTAU · QUALITY ASSURANCE

    Every batch independently verified

    Our laboratory partner is Hill Labs, Hamilton — one of New Zealand's most respected independent testing facilities and IANZ accredited (International Accreditation New Zealand).

    Every batch of Aho Farms product is tested before it enters supply. There are no shortcuts and no exceptions. We do not supply product without a Certificate of Analysis.

    The CoA covers: THC and CBD percentages, terpene profile, heavy metals, pesticide residues, microbial contamination, moisture content, and foreign matter. These are not spot checks — every batch receives a full panel.

    Prescribers and pharmacies can request batch-specific CoAs through the relevant portal. We maintain records of all historical CoAs.
