Dispensing Guidelines

Guidelines for pharmacists dispensing Aho Farms medicinal cannabis products. All products are Schedule 8 Controlled Drugs under New Zealand law.

Storage Requirements

  • Store below 25°C away from direct sunlight and moisture
  • Keep in original tamper-evident packaging until dispensed
  • Dispense in original packaging where possible
  • Follow your pharmacy's controlled drug storage protocols (Schedule 8)

Labelling Requirements

Standard NZ prescription labelling requirements apply. Labels must include:

  • Patient name
  • Prescriber name
  • Directions for use as prescribed
  • Date dispensed
  • Quantity dispensed
  • Batch number
  • Pharmacy name and contact details

Patient Counselling Points

  • Explain vaporisation method — do not combust or smoke the product
  • Onset: 5–15 minutes for inhalation, 30–90 minutes for oral administration
  • Advise not to drive or operate machinery until individual response is established
  • Store away from children and in a secure location
  • Do not share prescription medicine with others
  • Common effects: relaxation, altered perception, dry mouth, increased appetite
  • Contact prescriber if experiencing adverse effects

Controlled Drug Handling

These products are Schedule 8 Controlled Drugs under the Misuse of Drugs Act 1975. Refer to your pharmacy's controlled drug protocols for storage, recording, and dispensing requirements. Controlled drug registers must be maintained as required by law.

Returns Note

Dispensed product cannot be returned under any circumstances — this is a legal requirement. For undispensed stock returns, see the Returns & Claims section.

Returns & Claims

Returns and claims policy for Aho Farms pharmacy supply. Please read all sections carefully before initiating a return.

Returns Policy

Undispensed stock may be returned within 30 days of delivery in original unopened packaging. Contact pharmacies@ahofarms.co.nz before returning. A credit note will be issued on receipt and inspection of returned stock.

Damaged Goods

Report any damaged goods within 48 hours of delivery, including photos of the damage and packaging. A replacement will be dispatched or a credit note issued once the claim has been assessed.

Product Quality Complaints

Report any quality concerns immediately to pharmacies@ahofarms.co.nz. Batch number is required. We take all quality complaints seriously and investigate within 5 business days. Quality complaints may trigger a formal product recall process in accordance with Medsafe requirements.

Legal Requirement

Dispensed product cannot be returned under any circumstances. This is a requirement under the Medicines Act 1981 and applies without exception.
