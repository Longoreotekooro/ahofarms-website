# Aho Farms — Ethical Backlink Strategy

Date: 2026-09-22. Companion to the website audit and SEO report.

## Principles

- Earn links from organisations that would reasonably mention Aho Farms
  anyway: Māori business networks, the New Zealand medicinal cannabis
  sector, Hawke's Bay business and media, horticulture and research bodies,
  export and trade channels.
- No paid links, link exchanges, article spinning, private blog networks or
  low-quality directories. Every outreach must offer the target something
  genuine: a profile, a story, data, a partnership or a member listing.
- Each link should point at the page that best answers the reader's
  question, not always the home page.
- Nothing below names a confirmed relationship. Every row is a proposed
  target for Aho Farms to approve before outreach starts.

## Content that earns links without outreach

- **Terroir and cultivation explainers**: the 39th parallel, sunshine
  hours, soil and climate content on the Land & Cultivation page is unique
  and citable. Keep it factual and dated.
- **Regulatory explainers**: the news items on the NZ scheme and Medsafe
  guidance are the kind of content peers and journalists reference. Give
  each a permanent article page (the news cards currently have no detail
  pages).
- **Annual social impact summary**: jobs created, whānau employed, land
  developed. A dated annual page becomes a reference for Māori business
  reporting.

## What not to do

- Do not buy placements or "sponsored" links presented as editorial.
- Do not submit to generic web directories or cannabis link farms.
- Do not use guest-post networks or reciprocal link pages.
- Do not link to or from any site that makes medical claims Aho Farms
  cannot make itself.

## Measurement

- Track referring domains monthly in Google Search Console (Links report)
  once the property is verified.
- Record each outreach in a simple sheet: target, date, contact, status,
  resulting URL.
- Review quarterly; drop targets that would need paid or artificial links.
