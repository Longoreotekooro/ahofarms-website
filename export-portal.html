Import Permit Support

We provide full documentation to support your import permit application, including Certificates of Analysis, phytosanitary certificates, and supply agreements. Our compliance team will work directly with your regulatory affairs staff to ensure documentation meets your in-country requirements.

Australia

TGA import permit required. We provide all documentation required for your TGA import permit application, including product specifications, batch CoAs, and company compliance documentation.

United Kingdom

MHRA import licence required. Full batch documentation provided. We can supply product information in formats compatible with UK regulatory requirements.

European Union

Country-specific import requirements apply. Germany, Netherlands, and other EU markets each have distinct regulatory pathways. Contact us to discuss requirements specific to your market.

Compliance Contact: For documentation requests and regulatory enquiries, contact compliance@ahofarms.co.nz

Process

Partner Onboarding Process

Typical timeline: 4–8 weeks from initial enquiry to first order for new partners.

1

Initial Enquiry

Submit the partner interest form on our website. We review all enquiries within 3 business days and respond to qualifying partners with next steps.

2

Discovery Call

30-minute call with our export team to discuss your market, regulatory environment, volume requirements, and distribution capability.

3

Documentation Exchange

You provide: Company registration, import licence or application, regulatory approval in your market.
We provide: Capability statement, product specifications, batch CoAs, full compliance documentation.

4

Commercial Terms

Pricing proposal issued. Commercial terms discussed and agreed, including volumes, incoterms, currency, and payment structure.

5

Supply Agreement

Formal supply agreement signed by both parties. Agreement covers volumes, pricing, delivery terms, documentation obligations, and compliance requirements.

6

First Order

Pilot order placed and fulfilled with full documentation support. Your import permit, phytosanitary certificates, and CoAs provided in advance of shipment.

7

Ongoing Supply

Regular orders under your supply agreement. Advance planning for each harvest. Dedicated export contact for your account.

Ready to start? If you haven't already submitted a partner enquiry, visit our partner registration page to begin the process.
