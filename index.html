Our Story

From the whenua to the patient

Aho Farms was founded on a simple conviction: that the land which sustained our tīpuna can sustain our future. We cultivate premium medicinal cannabis on Māori freehold land in Raupunga, Hawke's Bay — where the soil, the climate, and the people are part of the product.

In partnership with the Waaka whānau, we have built a licensed medicinal cannabis operation that places Māori employment, whenua development, and tikanga at the centre of everything we do.

Tiakina te whenua, tiakina te iwi.
— Aho Farms founding kaupapa

Our Kaupapa

Land, people, and purpose — bound together

Aho Farms is not simply a business built on Māori land. It is a Māori enterprise built for Māori futures. Every decision — from how we treat the soil to how we hire, to how we share the returns — is governed by the obligations we hold to the whenua and the people.

Tiakina te whenua, tiakina te iwi.

Guard the land, guard the people.
