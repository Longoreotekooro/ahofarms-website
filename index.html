02 / THE LAND
TE WĀHI · SENSE OF PLACE
Ko te whenua te waiū mō ngā uri whakatipu.

Terroir, at 39° south.

Our growing site sits in northern Hawke's Bay — one of the sunniest regions in Aotearoa, shaped by coastal air off Te Matau-a-Māui and soil farmed by the same whānau for five generations.

Wine has a word for what place does to a plant. So does medicinal cannabis: everything our flower is begins with where it grows.

    05 / OUR SCIENCE
    TE PŪTAIAO ME TE MĀTAURANGA · SCIENCE MEETS TRADITION

    Two strands. One weave.

    Pharmaceutical-grade cultivation and mātauranga Māori are not in tension at Aho Farms. Like the two strands of a woven cord, each holds the other in place.

    Te Taha Māori — the inherited strand

    • Kaitiakitanga. The land is held in trust for future generations — soil decisions are made for the long term, never for a single season's yield.
    • Whenua knowledge. Five generations of farming this specific land — its winds, its frosts, its seasons — guides when and where we plant.
    • Natural cycles. Our plants follow the sun and the seasons, not a timer. Outdoor photoperiod cultivation is tikanga and technique at once.
    • He tangata. Whānau hands plant, tend, and harvest every crop. Care is a cultivation input.

    Te Taha Pūtaiao — the measured strand

    • Pheno-hunting. 89 mother plants evaluated across cultivars, narrowed to the 15 strongest expressions for production.
    • Integrated pest management. No synthetic pesticides, herbicides, or artificial growth enhancers — pressure managed with natural inputs.
    • Independent verification. Every batch tested by Hill Labs (IANZ-accredited): cannabinoids, terpenes, heavy metals, pesticides, microbials.
    • <200 CFU. New Zealand's microbial standard is stricter than Australia's. Our flower consistently meets it.

    TE WHAKAMĀTAU · PROVEN QUALITY

    Trust is grown here too.

    Every claim we make is licensed, tested, or documented — and available to verified partners.

    Licensed Cultivation

    Licensed medicinal cannabis cultivator under New Zealand's Medicinal Cannabis Scheme — grown, dried, and processed in Aotearoa.

    Independent Testing

    Every batch analysed by Hill Labs, Hamilton — IANZ accredited. Cannabinoids, terpenes, heavy metals, pesticides, microbials.

    NZ Microbial Standard

    CFU count below 200 — New Zealand's standard, stricter than Australia's. Our sun-grown flower consistently meets it.

    Export Pathway

    Export licence active — documented supply pathway for qualified international partners, beginning with Australia.

    Batch Documentation

    Full chain-of-custody from plant to pack. Certificates of analysis accompany every batch released to prescribers and pharmacies.

    Prescription Medicine

    Dispensed only through licensed prescribers and pharmacies. Never sold direct. Never marketed to patients or consumers.
