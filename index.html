Our Story Tiakina te whenua, tiakina te iwi.

Five generations of land. One generation to build on it differently.

Aho Farms was founded on a conviction that Māori land, combined with California-grade cultivation expertise, could produce medicinal cannabis that sets a new standard in Aotearoa and beyond.

In partnership with the Waaka whānau, we grow on Māori freehold land in Raupunga, Hawke's Bay — at 39°S latitude, sharing the same position on the globe as California's legendary Emerald Triangle. The climate, the soil, and the people make this land exceptional. We treat it accordingly.

Our Foundations

What makes Aho Farms different

Four pillars that define our approach to cultivation, community, and commercial responsibility.

I

Deeply connected to Hawke's Bay

Five-generation whānau farming ancestral lands of Te Hapuku. Hawke's Bay shares the global latitude of California's Emerald Triangle — outdoor quality at lower cost, in NZ's highest sunshine region.

II

Advanced cultivation methods

Soil rebuilt to California standards. Rigorous feeding, watering, and growing regimens. Pheno hunt across 15+ cultivar variations. Fast production scalability backed by NZ's world-class agricultural sector.

III

Leading toward something better

Hauora with our community, customers, and suppliers. Deep iwi connection. Committed to economic regeneration — repurposing unused Māori freehold land to create pathways to economic freedom.

IV

Uncompromising quality

CFU microbial count <200 — NZ's standard exceeds Australia's threshold. Wide range of terpene profiles. Consistent, reliable supply. Every batch with a full certificate of analysis before distribution.
