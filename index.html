MŌ MĀTOU · OUR STORY Tiakina te whenua, tiakina te iwi.

Five generations of land. One generation to build on it differently.

Aho Farms was founded on a conviction that Māori land, combined with California-grade cultivation expertise, could produce medicinal cannabis that sets a new standard in Aotearoa and beyond.

In partnership with the Waaka whānau, we grow on Māori freehold land in Raupunga, Hawke's Bay — at 39°S latitude, sharing the same position on the globe as California's legendary Emerald Triangle. The climate, the soil, and the people make this land exceptional. We treat it accordingly.

Ō MĀTOU UARA · OUR FOUNDATIONS

What makes Aho Farms different

Four pillars that define our approach to cultivation, community, and commercial responsibility.

I

Deeply connected to Hawke's Bay

Five-generation whānau farming ancestral lands of Te Hapuku. Hawke's Bay shares the global latitude of California's Emerald Triangle — outdoor quality in NZ's highest sunshine region.

II

Advanced cultivation methods

Soil rebuilt to California standards. Rigorous feeding and growing regimens. Pheno hunt across 15+ cultivar variations. Fast production scalability backed by NZ's agricultural sector.

III

Leading toward something better

Hauora with our community, customers, and suppliers. Deep iwi connection. Committed to economic regeneration — repurposing unused Māori freehold land for whānau futures.

IV

Uncompromising quality

CFU microbial count <200 — NZ's standard exceeds Australia's. Wide terpene profiles. Consistent, reliable supply. Full certificate of analysis before every distribution.
