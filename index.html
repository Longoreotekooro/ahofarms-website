Ō MĀTOU UARA · OUR FOUNDATIONS

What makes Aho Farms different

Four pillars that define our approach to cultivation, community, and commercial responsibility.

I

Deeply connected to Hawke's Bay

Five-generation whānau farming ancestral lands of Te Hapuku. Hawke's Bay is one of New Zealand's sunniest, most fertile regions — exceptional outdoor growing conditions.

II

Advanced cultivation methods

Soil rebuilt to California standards. Rigorous feeding and growing regimens. Pheno hunt across 89 mother plants narrowed to 15 strongest. Fast production scalability backed by NZ's agricultural sector.

III

Leading toward something better

Hauora with our community, customers, and suppliers. Deep iwi connection. Committed to economic regeneration — repurposing unused Māori freehold land for whānau futures.

IV

Uncompromising quality

CFU microbial count <200 — NZ's standard exceeds Australia's. Wide terpene profiles. Consistent, reliable supply. Full certificate of analysis before every distribution.
