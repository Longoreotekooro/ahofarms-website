TE PŪTAIAO ME TE MĀTAURANGA · SCIENCE MEETS TRADITION

Two strands. One weave.

Pharmaceutical-grade cultivation and mātauranga Māori are not in tension at Aho Farms. Like the two strands of a woven cord, each holds the other in place.

Te Taha Māori — the inherited strand

  • Kaitiakitanga. The land is held in trust for future generations — soil decisions are made for the long term, never for a single season's yield.
  • Whenua knowledge. Five generations of farming this specific land — its winds, its frosts, its seasons — guides when and where we plant.
  • Natural cycles. Our plants follow the sun and the seasons, not a timer. Outdoor photoperiod cultivation is tikanga and technique at once.
  • He tangata. Whānau hands plant, tend, and harvest every crop. Care is a cultivation input.

Te Taha Pūtaiao — the measured strand

  • Pheno-hunting. 89 mother plants evaluated across cultivars, narrowed to the 15 strongest expressions for production.
  • Integrated pest management. No synthetic pesticides, herbicides, or artificial growth enhancers — pressure managed with natural inputs.
  • Independent verification. Every batch tested by Hill Labs (IANZ-accredited): cannabinoids, terpenes, heavy metals, pesticides, microbials.
  • <200 CFU. New Zealand's microbial standard is stricter than Australia's. Our flower consistently meets it.
