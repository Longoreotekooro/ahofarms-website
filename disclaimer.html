Medical & Legal Disclaimer

Important information about this website and Aho Farms products

1. Prescription Medicine

All medicinal cannabis products cultivated and supplied by Aho Farms Limited are classified as prescription medicines under the Medicines Act 1981 and the Misuse of Drugs (Medicinal Cannabis) Regulations 2019.

Aho Farms products:

  • Are not available for purchase without a valid prescription from a registered medical practitioner or nurse practitioner authorised to prescribe;
  • Are not sold in retail outlets, pharmacies without prescription, online stores, or health food retailers;
  • Are not available for direct purchase from Aho Farms by consumers, patients, or caregivers;
  • Must be dispensed by a licensed pharmacy following a valid prescription.

Any person or entity purporting to supply Aho Farms products outside these channels does not have our authorisation and may be acting unlawfully.

2. Not Medical Advice

Nothing on this website is medical advice. Aho Farms is a licensed medicinal cannabis cultivator and supplier. We are not a healthcare provider, and we do not provide clinical services.

We do not diagnose medical conditions. We do not advise patients on whether medicinal cannabis is appropriate for their circumstances. We do not recommend specific products, doses, or treatment regimens.

Product information published in the Aho Farms Prescriber Portal — including formulation data, cannabinoid profiles, indicative dosing ranges, and clinical reference materials — is provided to support clinical decision-making by qualified healthcare professionals. It is a reference resource and is not intended to replace clinical judgement, patient assessment, or the prescriber's obligations under applicable professional and regulatory standards.

If you are a patient, please consult your doctor, specialist, or a registered cannabis clinic before making any decision about medicinal cannabis.

3. No Therapeutic Claims

Aho Farms makes no therapeutic claims about its products. We do not claim that our products treat, cure, prevent, or manage any medical condition or disease.

Where this website or our materials reference medical conditions — such as chronic pain, sleep disorders, nausea, spasticity, or anxiety — these references are factual statements about conditions for which medicinal cannabis is commonly prescribed by clinicians in New Zealand, based on published prescribing data and clinical guidance. Such references are informational and contextual only.

They do not constitute a representation that any Aho Farms product is approved, indicated, or proven effective for any condition. Prescribers should form their own clinical judgement based on the current evidence base, individual patient circumstances, and applicable clinical guidelines.

4. Advertising Compliance

This website is operated in compliance with:

  • The Medicines Act 1981 — including restrictions on advertising prescription medicines to the general public;
  • The Misuse of Drugs (Medicinal Cannabis) Regulations 2019;
  • Medsafe advertising guidance for medicinal cannabis (current as of July 2025);
  • The Advertising Standards Authority (ASA) codes applicable to therapeutic and health advertising.

Aho Farms does not advertise its prescription medicinal cannabis products to the general public, consumers, or patients. All product-level information — including product names, cannabinoid specifications, formulation details, and pricing — is restricted to verified healthcare professionals in our Prescriber Portal.

If you believe any content on this website may be inconsistent with our advertising obligations, please contact our compliance team at compliance@ahofarms.co.nz.

5. Regulatory Status — New Zealand

Aho Farms Limited holds licences issued by the Ministry of Health under the New Zealand Medicinal Cannabis Scheme, including:

  • A Cultivation Licence authorising the cultivation of cannabis for medicinal purposes;
  • An Export Licence authorising the export of medicinal cannabis products to eligible overseas markets.

Our products are cultivated, processed, and supplied in accordance with licence conditions, including compliance with the Medicinal Cannabis Agency's minimum quality standards as specified in the Misuse of Drugs (Medicinal Cannabis) Regulations 2019.

Regulatory status is current as of the date shown on this page and is subject to change. Licence details are publicly available on the Ministry of Health's Medicinal Cannabis Agency register. We encourage interested parties to verify our current licence status directly with the Agency.

6. Regulatory Status — Australia

Aho Farms products may be made available to Australian patients through export, subject to applicable regulatory requirements. Australian prescribers and pharmacists should be aware that:

  • Importation of medicinal cannabis into Australia requires a valid import permit issued by the Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA);
  • Australian patients cannot access imported medicinal cannabis products without a valid prescription from an Australian-registered prescriber;
  • Products must be dispensed through a TGA-compliant pharmacy;
  • Prescribers must comply with TGA requirements for prescribing unapproved therapeutic goods, including the Special Access Scheme (SAS) or Authorised Prescriber (AP) pathway as applicable.

Australian prescribers should refer to current TGA guidance on prescribing and importing medicinal cannabis. Aho Farms does not provide regulatory advice for Australian prescribers. For TGA guidance, visit www.tga.gov.au.
