03 / THE NAME
INGOA · THE NAME

More than a name.
A philosophy.

Aho is the thread. In te reo Māori, it is the cord that connects, the line that holds, and the bond that binds people to each other and to the land. Our name was chosen deliberately — not as branding, but as belief.

Every decision we make, every relationship we build, and every product we grow is an expression of that thread. The aho between people and whenua. Between past and future. Between healing knowledge and those who need it.

Aho
Thread / Connection

The horizontal threads of woven flax — the aho holds the structure together. Without the aho, there is no weaving. Without connection, there is no company.

Whenua

Our operation is rooted in Hawke's Bay — ancestral land that gives identity, purpose, and sustenance. The whenua is not a resource. It is a responsibility.

Māra
Garden / Cultivation

The māra is the place where knowledge meets earth — where the discipline of cultivation becomes an act of care. Aho Farms is a māra with purpose and precision.

04 / HE KŌRERO
HE KŌRERO · OUR STORY

From the soil up.
Built to last.

Aho Farms did not begin with a spreadsheet. It began with a conviction — that Māori land should produce Māori prosperity, and that the knowledge to make that happen already existed. It just needed to find its way home.

Chapter One

The return.
From California to Hawke's Bay.

The founder of Aho Farms spent years inside California's regulated cannabis industry — rising from cultivation floors to overseeing teams of 23+, managing full harvest cycles, and building an understanding of what genuine quality control looks like at scale. It was not a detour. It was preparation.

Returning to Aotearoa, the conviction was clear: the knowledge gap between California and New Zealand represented an opportunity. Not an opportunity to import American culture, but to apply hard-earned expertise to Māori land, under Māori governance, for Māori futures. The expertise came from California. The purpose came from home.

"The knowledge came from California. The purpose came from home."
Chapter Two

The whenua.
Land as the foundation.

Aho Farms was established on Māori freehold land in Hawke's Bay — a partnership built not simply on commercial terms, but on the obligations of kaitiakitanga. The land was not chosen for convenience. It was chosen because it was ours to care for, and because productive use of that land in Māori hands represents something far greater than any single harvest.

The partnership created employment pathways for local whānau, activated land that had not been in productive use, and demonstrated what Māori land-based enterprise can look like when it is done properly — compliant, commercially serious, and deeply connected to the people it belongs to.

"Productive use of Māori land, in Māori hands — that is what this has always been about."
Chapter Three

The licence.
Compliance as credibility.

New Zealand's Medicinal Cannabis Scheme has among the strictest quality standards in the world — a regulatory environment designed to protect patients and separate genuine operators from those who simply want proximity to a growing market. Earning a cultivation licence is not a formality. It is proof of intent.

Aho Farms is one of a limited number of licensed medicinal cannabis cultivators in New Zealand. Every batch is independently laboratory tested. Every product meets or exceeds the scheme's stringent microbial and cannabinoid requirements. That compliance is not a constraint we work around. It is the standard we hold ourselves to — and the guarantee we make to every prescriber, pharmacist, and patient in the supply chain.

"Compliance is not a constraint. It is the commitment we make to every patient in our supply chain."

TE WHAKAMĀTAU · PROVEN QUALITY

Trust is grown here too.

Every claim we make is licensed, tested or documented, and available to verified partners.

Licensed medicinal cannabis cultivator under New Zealand's Medicinal Cannabis Scheme, with every crop grown, dried and processed in Aotearoa.

Independent Testing

Every batch analysed by IANZ accredited Hill Labs in Hamilton. Cannabinoids, terpenes, heavy metals, pesticides and microbials.
