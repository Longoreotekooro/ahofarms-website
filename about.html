OUR STORY

Cultivating ancestral wisdom

A Māori-led enterprise rooted in Hawke's Bay land, guided by tikanga, and growing for the future of our whānau and our patients.

THE BEGINNING

From California expertise to Hawke's Bay whenua

Aho Farms was founded by Longo Reo Te Kooro, a Māori entrepreneur who spent years in California's medicinal and recreational cannabis industry — rising from the floor to managing 23+ staff and overseeing full harvest, manicuring, and packaging operations. He returned to Aotearoa with a clear conviction: that the knowledge and systems he had developed in the world's most mature cannabis market could be applied on ancestral land, for his own people.

In partnership with the Waaka whānau, Aho Farms was established on Māori freehold land in Raupunga, Hawke's Bay. The partnership was not simply commercial — it was an expression of what Māori land-based enterprise should look like: productive, compliant, and deeply connected to the obligations of kaitiakitanga.

Today, Aho Farms holds a cultivation licence under New Zealand's Medicinal Cannabis Scheme, grows two medicinal flower product lines, and is building the supply infrastructure to reach prescribers, pharmacies, and export markets. The foundation is the whenua. Everything else is built on top of it.

"Tiakina te whenua, tiakina te iwi." — Founding kaupapa of Aho Farms
MILESTONES

Our journey

2020

Company founded. Partnership formed with Waaka whānau on Māori freehold land, Raupunga, Hawke's Bay. The whenua provides the commercial and cultural foundation of the enterprise.

2021

Licence applications lodged. Site preparation and compliance infrastructure development begins. Ministry of Health engagement commenced.

2022

Cultivation licence granted under the NZ Medicinal Cannabis Scheme. A material milestone — one of 42 granted in New Zealand.

2023

First cultivation season. Michael Maclean joins as Cultivation Director, bringing 15+ years of California cannabis cultivation expertise to the Raupunga operation.

2024

First harvest completed. Laboratory testing partnership established with Hill Labs, Hamilton (IANZ accredited). Board of Directors formalised with independent chair Stephen McPhail.

2025

First supply to NZ prescribers. RĀ and SOURCE product lines launched. Prescriber portal established. Product available through pharmacy channel on prescription.

2026

Export licence active. Second successful harvest season. Distribution partnerships under development for Australian and international markets.

Longo Reo Te Kooro

Managing Director & Founder

Longo Reo Te Kooro is a Māori entrepreneur from Hawke's Bay with over a decade of experience in the cannabis industry, including several years in California's regulated recreational and medicinal market. He rose from entry-level cultivation to managing teams of 23+, overseeing harvest, manicuring, and packaging at scale.

Returning to Aotearoa, he recognised that the knowledge gap between California and New Zealand represented an opportunity — and that the land, the people, and the regulatory moment were aligned. Aho Farms is the result.

His focus: bringing international cultivation expertise to bear on Māori land, under Māori governance, for Māori futures.
