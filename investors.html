WHY AHO FARMS

The investment case

Land & Licence

Cultivation on Māori freehold land in Hawke's Bay gives Aho structural cost and security advantages unavailable to non-Māori operators. Combined with an active cultivation licence, this creates a defensible base that competitors cannot simply replicate with capital alone.

Market Timing

NZ and Australian medicinal cannabis markets are in early growth phase. Australia alone has 1M+ patients, 80,000+ monthly prescriptions, and growing prescriber adoption. We are positioned ahead of scale, building supply relationships before demand consolidates.

Product Quality

IANZ-accredited lab testing, documented CoA process, and cultivation across indoor, greenhouse and outdoor environments differentiate Aho product in a market where quality consistency is a primary buyer concern. Every batch is tested and documented to export-grade standards.

Cultural Capital

Māori ownership creates access to government support, iwi investment networks, Māori procurement preferences, and a brand story that no competitor can replicate. This is a structural commercial advantage rooted in ownership — not marketing.
