Our Proposition

The case for New Zealand-grown medicinal cannabis

Quality Advantage

  • Hawke's Bay flower — indoor, greenhouse and outdoor sun-grown
  • IANZ-accredited Hill Labs testing
  • Documented chain of custody seed to lab
  • Batch CoAs available for every supply

Regulatory Status

  • Licensed under NZ Misuse of Drugs Act
  • Full compliance with NZ Medicinal Cannabis Scheme
  • Export licensing in place
  • UN Convention compliant supply documentation

Supply Reliability

  • Two established cultivation sites in Hawke's Bay
  • Consistent growing season
  • Cold chain-capable packaging
  • Responsive to partner volume requirements

Target Markets

Where we supply

🇦🇺

Australia

TGA-regulated market. 1M+ patients, 80,000+ prescriptions per month. Strong demand for quality-documented supply.

🇳🇿

New Zealand

Domestic market. 42 licensed operators. Growing prescriber base. Local supply advantage.

🇬🇧

United Kingdom

MHRA regulated. Growing medicinal cannabis market. Strong demand for premium quality-documented product.

🇪🇺

Europe

Multiple regulatory pathways. Germany largest market post-legalisation. Seeking reliable supply partners.

We work with qualified distributors in each market. Direct supply subject to bilateral treaty compliance and in-market regulatory requirements.
